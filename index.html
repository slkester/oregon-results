Current results come from the Associated Press election data behind news-outlet result embeds (race calls and AP's estimate of the expected vote counted) and from the Oregon Secretary of State's unofficial results. Oregon counts ballots postmarked by Election Day that arrive within seven days, so margins move for a week or more. Counties certify in about three weeks; the Secretary of State certifies in early December.
